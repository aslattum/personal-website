Capital One Mobile is one of the top-rated finance apps on the App Store, letting millions of customers manage their cards and accounts, pay bills, check their credit with CreditWise, and send money with Zelle.

- Started as an Associate Software Engineer and was promoted to Software Engineer after a year, working across the flagship mobile apps and the platforms behind them.
- Led the architecture and development of an internal framework for server-driven UI layouts powered by machine learning models.
- Developed a reactive programming framework, built on Redux principles, that feature teams adopted to improve testability and consistency, and helped lead the move from RxSwift to Combine.
- Built CI/CD pipelines with Jenkins and fastlane for the iOS and Android apps, plus serverless dashboards (AWS Lambda, RDS, Superset) to monitor build health.
- Shipped full-stack features with Vue.js and Node.js.
